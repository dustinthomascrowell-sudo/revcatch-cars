Put it on your home screen

No app store needed. Took the shot on your phone camera while riding along? Upload it within 48 hours and it still counts. Thirty seconds and it opens like any other app.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots, top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install.
Passengers and parked cars only. Never use the app while you drive. The camera locks when the phone is moving at highway speed.
